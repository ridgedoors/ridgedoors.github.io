Common questions

Is a steel door cheaper than a wood door in the long run?

In most coastal homes, yes. Wood usually costs less upfront, but recurring polish, termite treatment and humidity repair mean steel typically becomes the cheaper option within 8 to 10 years.

Do steel doors rust in coastal areas like Kanyakumari?

Powder-coated steel doors are treated specifically for salt-air exposure and resist rusting far better than untreated metal. Periodic repainting every few years maintains this protection.

How often does a wood door need termite treatment?

In humid coastal regions, wood doors typically need termite treatment every 2 to 3 years, along with periodic polishing and occasional repair for moisture-related warping.
