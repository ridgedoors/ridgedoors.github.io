Questions we hear often

What is the difference between a pre-hung door and an FRP door?

A pre-hung door is made with a WPC composite panel, PU foam and an FRP skin, and comes with a frame. An FRP door is a fibre door made of glass fibre and resin. The pre-hung designs on this page can also be supplied as FRP doors; ask us if you want one in FRP.

Can I use these doors in a bathroom?

Yes. Pre-hung doors come in a toilet-door size (8.5 × 4.3 cm frame, 28 mm door) as well as a bedroom size. Tell us where the door will go and we will suggest the right type.

How much do they cost?

The price depends on the door type, size, finish and hardware. Send us the design name and your door size on WhatsApp and we will quote.

Yes. We supply across Kanyakumari district, including Nagercoil, Marthandam and Colachel, and we welcome enquiries from Tirunelveli too.
